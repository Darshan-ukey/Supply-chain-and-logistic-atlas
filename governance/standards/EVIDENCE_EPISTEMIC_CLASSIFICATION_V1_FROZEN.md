# Evidence / Epistemic Classification Vocabulary V1 — FROZEN

Status: **FROZEN GOVERNING VOCABULARY**  
Effective: 2026-09-02

Every operational or execution claim must carry an epistemic class.

- **SOURCE_EXPLICIT** — directly and explicitly supported by the cited authoritative source.
- **SOURCE_DERIVED** — logically derived from explicit source statements; derivation must be documented.
- **ATLAS_DERIVED** — Atlas synthesis/normalization across governed evidence; not represented as direct source wording.
- **CLIENT_POLICY** — organization-specific policy/threshold/operating choice supplied by a client or operator.
- **CLIENT_BINDING** — environment-specific system, field, team, route, code, contract, master-data or configuration binding.
- **UNKNOWN** — unresolved; must remain visible and must not be fabricated.

Optional execution-specific subclasses may further distinguish CLIENT_SYSTEM_BINDING, CONTRACT_PARAMETER, REGULATORY_CONTEXT, TRANSACTION_VALUE, MASTER_DATA, DERIVED_VALUE, and SYSTEM_GENERATED, but they do not replace the governing epistemic class.

A claim may only be promoted to canonical Atlas truth when its evidence class, provenance, applicability and confidence have passed the applicable governance gate.
