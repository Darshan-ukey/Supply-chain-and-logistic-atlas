## Decisive governing-standard check

The frozen `EXECUTABILITY_AND_RECURSIVE_DECOMPOSITION_STANDARD_V1_FROZEN.md` requires an executable unit to define or explicitly bind:
- entry trigger and required pre-state;
- required information/business objects and validation;
- applicability/context;
- decision logic and authority boundary;
- rules/controls;
- permitted action;
- branch/state transition for each material result;
- timing/wait semantics where applicable;
- exception/retry/escalation/recovery behavior;
- evidence/output and completion criteria;
- system/actor authority or an explicit binding requirement.

It also states that a unit cannot be marked executable if a required decision, state transition, evidence requirement, exception path, or binding dependency is implicit.

The frozen compiler specification further requires, for every `EXECUTOR_READY` leaf, sufficient source material to identify required input/object, entry/applicability, decision/gate logic without undocumented judgement, one unambiguous operation, success/failure condition, required evidence, resulting state/transition and resolved canonical authority/role.
