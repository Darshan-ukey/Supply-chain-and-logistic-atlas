## 5. Executability challenge (Step D) — THE DECISIVE FINDING

Per the handoff's explicit instruction, *"explicitly challenge all 38 currently ready leaves. The
burden of proof is on EXECUTOR_READY."* Quantified detail-field coverage precisely across the 38
`EXECUTOR_READY` leaves specifically (not all 64 units):

| Detail field | Populated on ready leaves |
|---|---|
| `atomicActions` | 7 / 38 |
| `decisionGates` | 5 / 38 |
| `trigger` | **0 / 38** |
| `evidenceRequirements` | 3 / 38 |
| `outputState` | **0 / 38** |
| `name` identical to `purpose` (no added specificity) | 22 / 38 |
| **Zero of the 5 fields above populated at all** | **24 / 38 (63%)** |

Even where `atomicActions` is populated, it is frequently a single-item list that merely restates
the unit's own name (e.g. `WD-LTL03-04A`: name *"Instantiate canonical TransportDocument"*,
`atomicActions: ["Instantiate canonical TransportDocument"]`) rather than a genuinely specified
operation.

The V1 test this handoff itself specifies requires, for every `EXECUTOR_READY` leaf: (1) required
input/object, (2) entry/applicability condition, (3) decision/gate logic, (4) one unambiguous
operation, (5) success/failure condition, (6) required evidence, (7) resulting state/transition,
(8) resolved canonical authority/role.

**Items (2) and (7) — entry/applicability condition and resulting state/transition — are not
demonstrated for a single one of the 38 ready leaves.** Item (4) is genuinely demonstrated for only
7 of 38, and even those are frequently name-restatements rather than specified operations. Item (5)
is not demonstrated anywhere in the artifact for any leaf.

This is not a handful of isolated gaps. It is the majority condition of the "ready" set.

**Comparison control:** the 10 blocked leaves correctly show zero action/gate/evidence detail
(expected — blocked units aren't supposed to be executable yet), confirming this is not simply
"the schema doesn't carry this field" — the schema and the artifact both support these fields
(6/38 ready leaves DO have real `atomicActions`), they are simply absent for the large majority of
units the artifact itself classifies as ready to execute.

## 12. Final disposition

**`P6_1_V1_LTL03_RECONSTRUCTION__INDEPENDENT_QA_FAIL`**

Not a structural or schema failure — those are genuinely sound, independently confirmed. Not a
source-authority overstatement — the grounding is real, specific, and in several cases verified
down to exact matching claim IDs. The failure is narrower and specific: **the artifact declares 38
leaves `EXECUTOR_READY` without carrying the executability evidence (trigger, unambiguous
operation, success/failure condition, evidence, output state) the V1 test itself requires for that
classification**, and this gap affects the majority of the ready set (24/38 with zero of five
detail fields; 0/38 for two of those fields specifically) rather than a small, boundable number of
units.

This is a **broad completeness gap, not a redesign problem**: the skeleton, hierarchy, blocker
classification, and source-grounding approach do not need to be redone. What is missing is the
per-leaf executability detail that would let a reviewer actually verify — rather than infer from a
plausible-sounding name — that each "ready" leaf is genuinely ready.

**Recommended path forward, not a broad rebuild:** populate `trigger`, a genuine (non-name-
duplicating) `atomicActions` entry, a success/failure condition, `evidenceRequirements`, and
`outputState` for the 24 bare leaves at minimum (workUnitIds: WD-LTL03-05A, 05B, 06A, 07A, 07B,
08A, 08B, 08C, 09A, 09B, 09C, 09D, 09E, 09F, 10A, 10C, 15A, 15B, 15C, 16B, 17B, 18A, 18B, 19B),
each still strictly source-grounded against the same four pinned files — then resubmit for a
second independent pass focused specifically on the newly-added detail. Also recommend upgrading
`sourceRefs` from file-level to claim-level citations where the source material supports it
(confirmed it does, at least for the hazmat family).
