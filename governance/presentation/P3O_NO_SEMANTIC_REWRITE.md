# P3O No-Semantic-Rewrite Decision

The Universal Daughter Renderer architecture does not require Ocean FCL/LCL semantic rewrites. Therefore P3O may only materialize and register exact frozen Ocean 0.6 assets and certify them through the P2/P3 path. Any future semantic enrichment, including explicit Operational Knowledge v2 / Information Resolution enrichment, must create a new Ocean version rather than modifying 0.6.
