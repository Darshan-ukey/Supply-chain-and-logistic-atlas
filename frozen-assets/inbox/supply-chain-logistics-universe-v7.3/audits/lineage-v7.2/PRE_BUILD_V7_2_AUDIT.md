# Universe V7.2 — Pre-Build Audit

## Objective

Second correction-cycle release after Ocean FCL/LCL v0.3. The release must improve the parent/source foundation without changing the established Page-0 composition architecture or v1.1.8 product behavior.

## Baseline architecture to preserve

The exact V6.2.3 release donor continues to expose:

- 71 navigator destinations
- 27 enterprise lenses
- 9 business cycles
- 51 context resolver rules
- 728 compatibility combinations
- 17 authority domains
- 12 movement patterns
- 15 logistics nodes

Universe V7.1 preserves those same structural counts. V7.2 must do the same.

## Outside-in candidate review

### Promote

1. **UN/CEFACT Visibility and Collaborative Planning in Multimodal Supply Chain Operations BRS v1.0** — published 3 Jul 2026. Parent-level value: cross-modal Transport Event Registry semantics plus governed access/delegation for multimodal visibility/collaboration. Boundary: does not replace DCSA/GS1/mode-specific execution standards and does not prescribe one universal execution sequence.

### Refresh status only — do not promote replacement semantics

2. **CTU Code** — UNECE still presents the 2014 Code as the published standards-page baseline. The 2025 revision is available through 2026 finalization documents. Keep 2014 active and mark revision as `FINALIZATION_IN_PROGRESS_NOT_PROMOTED`.

3. **UN Convention on Negotiable Cargo Documents** — adopted 15 Dec 2025, scheduled to open for signature on 26 Oct 2026, and not yet in force as of the latest UN Treaty Collection status checked 29 Aug 2026. Refresh source status only; do not change legal applicability.

## Build constraint

No daughter A3/A4/A5 content is promoted into Universe V7.2. No change to the 71-destination taxonomy, compatibility lattice, canonical entity IDs, relationship vocabulary, directional exchanges, or UI interaction model is authorized in this correction cycle.
