Two-direction discovery · full enterprise envelope

Find white space without declaring it too early

Top-down outcomes and bottom-up execution friction meet at the selected process boundary. A mode may emphasize its Execution Core, but it never removes the broader enterprise dependencies that can cause, constrain, monetize or receive consequences from execution.

Top-down: outcome and control

Enterprise outcome → lens → mode/service → process → node → decision/control → execution evidence.

Bottom-up: event, handoff and exception

Execution event/problem → process → node → mode → upstream/downstream domains → enterprise outcome.

Root-cause versus failure-location rule

The location where a failure becomes visible is not necessarily the process where the root cause originated. Trace across nodes, roles, systems, objects and upstream/downstream domains before assigning ownership.

Freight reclassification · visible in rating/billingPossible root: product master, dimensions, weight or classification.
Missed pickup · visible in transportPossible root: capacity planning, provider allocation or dock readiness.
Damage claim · visible in claimsPossible root: packaging, node handling, terminal operation or securement.
Billing dispute · visible in financePossible root: contract, rate, execution evidence or master data.

Diagnostic questions for the neutral ecosystem

    Guardrail: A gap in visibility, automation or data quality is not proof of value. Validate frequency, materiality, root cause, owner, existing controls and economics before design.

    Orientation

    What this Universe is—and what it is not

    A global-neutral supply-chain, logistics and distribution reference architecture that keeps source-native frameworks, Atlas crosswalks, execution overlays, enterprise lenses, systems/objects and evidence boundaries distinguishable.

    In simple English: Use the Universe as a governed map of how supply-chain work fits together: what happens, who does it, which systems/objects/evidence are involved, which sources support the statement, and how context changes execution.
    Non-claim: A claim that one framework contains the whole Atlas · A legal opinion · A universal regulatory applicability engine · A client-specific operating model unless client evidence is mapped · A claim that every Atlas A3/A4/A5 label is source-native
    Methodology

    How the Universe and verified daughter modules come to life

    The sequence below is the governed construction logic. It distinguishes what comes from source frameworks from what the Atlas crosswalks or synthesizes.

    1

    Preserve source-native process architecture

    SCOR and APQC remain independent native frameworks. Their published semantics are shown/crosswalked rather than silently rewritten as Atlas-native taxonomy.

    In simple English: Start with established process frameworks instead of inventing a new supply-chain language and pretending it came from an industry body.

    2

    Separate modelling notation from process evidence

    BPMN and DMN are modelling/decision notations. They can represent workflow and decision logic later, but they are not logistics process-content evidence.

    In simple English: A diagramming language can show a process; it does not prove that the process itself is an industry standard.

    3

    Add semantic/object/interoperability standards

    UN/CEFACT, UN/EDIFACT, GS1 and related standards provide business-object, identity, event and message semantics within their published scope.

    In simple English: These sources help define what the data, documents, identifiers and events mean across organizations and systems.

    4

    Add mode, role and legal/regulatory source packs conditionally

    Mode/role/legal references are applied only within their actual mode, jurisdiction, contract, goods and carriage scope. Inclusion in the source universe never means universal applicability.

    In simple English: A rule can be authoritative without applying to every shipment. The Atlas keeps the rule available but activates it only when the real-world context qualifies.

    5

    Create Atlas canonical navigation and crosswalks

    Atlas domains/lenses/navigation organize the source material. Crosswalks are explicitly Atlas interpretations and may be one-to-many.

    In simple English: The Atlas gives users one coherent way to navigate many different standards without falsely claiming the standards use the same structure.

    6

    Build execution children under the Universe parent

    Detailed daughter A4/A5 records are Atlas child synthesis inherited from the Universe and bounded issuer evidence. They are not claimed as source-native SCOR/APQC nodes.

    In simple English: The Universe is the governed parent map; each verified daughter module is a detailed operating model built under it using authoritative sources and explicit synthesis.

    7

    Separate ontology from process flow

    Typed entities (state, event, decision, rule, control, action, evidence, outcome, actor, object) and their ontology relationships are governed separately from process-flow edges such as PRECEDES, REQUIRES, TRIGGERS or RECOVERS_TO.

    In simple English: What something *is* is different from what happens *before or after* it. The Atlas keeps those two questions separate.

    8

    Keep provenance, claim boundaries and confidence visible

    Every detailed record carries provenance/source mapping/claim boundary/confidence so users can distinguish source-native evidence, Atlas synthesis and unresolved research.

    In simple English: The Atlas should be able to answer not only ‘what does this say?’ but also ‘where did this come from and how certain are we?’

    9

    Compose execution context without deleting the enterprise Universe

    Mode/service is the primary execution lens; role, movement, node, jurisdiction, carriage regime, condition and contract/service refine applicability. Enterprise lenses remain analytical views over the same governed universe.

    In simple English: Choose the operating situation you care about, but do not pretend the rest of the enterprise stops existing.

    10

    Treat gaps as hypotheses, not conclusions

    A visible failure or automation gap is not proof of root cause or value. Trace upstream/downstream dependencies, evidence, controls, frequency, materiality and economics before design.

    In simple English: Where a problem shows up is often not where it started. Investigate before proposing automation or AI.

    11

    Run the outside-in correction loop for every daughter module

    Build each daughter from the current frozen Universe plus authoritative domain sources. If daughter research exposes a generic parent gap or source-version gap, route it to the next governed Universe/source release; keep domain-specific execution detail in the daughter module.

    In simple English: The Universe is not a one-time mapping exercise. Each new domain makes both the daughter module and the parent/source foundation stronger without silently rewriting a frozen release.

    What different source families contribute

    Truth & origin

    Source-native, crosswalk, synthesis and runtime state are different things

    SOURCE-NATIVE

    Content represented as belonging to the issuer's own framework/standard vocabulary.

    In real life: This label/structure genuinely comes from the named source framework.

    SOURCE-NATIVE CROSSWALK

    Atlas mapping from a canonical/enterprise construct to source-native nodes while preserving source identity.

    In real life: The Atlas links its navigation to the framework; it does not claim the framework uses Atlas labels.

    REFERENCE-DERIVED CROSSWALK

    Atlas analytical mapping derived from reference sources where no single source-native hierarchy is claimed.

    In real life: Multiple references support the idea, but the final grouping is an Atlas interpretation.

    ATLAS ANALYTICAL / CONTROL CONSTRUCT

    Atlas-created analytical/control lens, explicitly not claimed as source-native taxonomy.

    In real life: This is a useful way to analyze the enterprise, but it is our analytical construct rather than an industry framework node.

    ATLAS CHILD SYNTHESIS

    Detailed child execution model inherited from Universe and bounded issuer evidence.

    In real life: The detailed operating flow is assembled by the Atlas from its parent structure and multiple authoritative sources; no single framework is claimed to own the whole flow.

    RUNTIME / PRESENTATION STATE

    UI/composition/trace state generated by the Atlas runtime; not source evidence.

    In real life: This is how the interface is showing relevance or status right now, not an industry classification.

    Rule: A supporting source does not make an Atlas-synthesized process a source-native node. Provenance tells you how the Atlas statement was created; source IDs tell you which bounded evidence supports it.

    Client operating model

    Reference model is not client truth

    CONFIRMED

    Human/client/SME validated client fact.

    OBSERVED

    Operational/client evidence supports the fact, but explicit human confirmation may still be pending.

    PUBLICLY_EVIDENCED

    Credible external/public evidence supports it; not the same as client confirmation.

    INFERRED

    Reasoned hypothesis from known facts; must never auto-promote.

    REFERENCE_ONLY

    Atlas says the component exists in the reference model; client-specific reality is unknown.

    UNKNOWN

    No reliable client-specific fact is known.

    How the chatbot should explain the Atlas

    First classify the exact Atlas thing and its provenance; then use Gemini to explain the real-world meaning. If more than one namespace is valid, ask a clarification question instead of guessing.

    Default answer: Simple English → Why it matters → Where it fits → Atlas classification → Origin/provenance → Evidence/source → Applicability/confidence → optional Trace / Show source / Compare.
    Example: OTHER_LTL_KNOWLEDGE is a runtime trace-relevance state. In simple English, it means “other parts of the governed LTL model that still exist but are not directly involved in the thing you are tracing right now.” It is not a jurisdiction or source category.
