## Epistemic boundary
1. **Atlas Reference** — governed source-backed reference content.
2. **Client Confirmed** — explicit client evidence/validation attached.
3. **Client Observed** — observed evidence, not necessarily formally validated.
4. **Client Inferred** — explicit hypothesis/rationale; not client truth.
5. **Unknown** — no client evidence; the system does not auto-fill from the reference.

## Deviation rule
A difference from the Atlas reference is recorded as a **deviation** only. It requires separate validation before it can be classified as a gap, risk or transformation opportunity.
