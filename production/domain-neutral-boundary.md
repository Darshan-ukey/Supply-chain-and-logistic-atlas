# Enterprise Core / Supply Chain Domain boundary

The generic runtime knows: Capability, Process, Task, State, Event, Decision, Rule, Control, Action, Evidence, Outcome, Actor/Role, System, Business Object, Relationship and Provenance.

Supply Chain registers its own territories, applicability dimensions, signals and heuristics. The Accounts-Payable fixture is deliberately non-publishable; it exists only to prove that the core engine does not require Shipment, Mode, Physical flow or LTL semantics.
